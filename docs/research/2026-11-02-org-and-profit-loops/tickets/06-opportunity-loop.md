# 06: The opportunity loop

Phase: 2

Repository: `chanjdaniel/agent-company`, base `dev`.

**What to build:** the weekly strategy session, a fresh Claude Code session that is not a cell, which reads the ledger, the evidence the cells gathered and the rejected-proposal list, and writes up to three opportunity memos, each with a hypothesis, the cheapest possible test, its budgets in tokens, dollars and Daniel-minutes, a pre-registered threshold, and its evidence cited to the ledger or to outside sources.
The portfolio rule ranks the memos; Daniel admits each new bet in the daily batch.

**Blocked by:** 02

- [ ] A memo whose claims cite nothing is refused by the Treasurer's checker and logged.
- [ ] A memo that repeats a rejected proposal without new evidence is refused, and the rejected list says which.
- [ ] An admitted bet enters the ledger with its trial budgets and its first outside-facing experiment's registration, and the memo's author session is not the one that runs it.
- [ ] Without Daniel's admission in the ledger, no memo becomes a bet; a test covers it.
- [ ] One real strategy session runs on the real ledger inside the governor's share and its memos reach Daniel's batch.
